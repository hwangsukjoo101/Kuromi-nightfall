## Final prompt set

### Ship deck
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background for an overhead browser survival game, landscape 1536x1024 aspect 3:2. Create a photorealistic AAA videogame render of a pirate ship's broad rectangular wooden battle deck, viewed directly from above, orthographic camera, no horizon. Weathered rich teak planks, detailed grain, brass nails, salt stains and warm late-afternoon sun from upper left. Deep turquoise ocean with realistic foam fills a narrow perimeter around all four sides. Deck fills rectangle x=5% to95%, y=15% to89%. Main playable deck must be CLEAR and OPEN, uninterrupted flat planks, no masts, rails across middle, crates, characters or objects in playable center. Rope coils and brass mooring cleats only at extreme deck corners. Thin ship gunwales at boundary. Natural cinematic light, tactile materials, subtle ambient shadows, high detail but restrained contrast so moving characters stay readable. No text, no UI, no letters, no logos, no watermark.

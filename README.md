# 🏴‍☠️ One Piece: Grand Line Survival

**A One Piece pirate survival game starring Luffy. Battle waves of Marines, unlock seven weapons, and activate Gear 5—all in your browser.**

## [▶ Play One Piece: Grand Line Survival](https://hwangsukjoo101.github.io/Onepiece-vs-marines/?v=dark-hole)

### The adventure

Defend a wooden ship deck against endless Marine waves. Start with three lives, collect pink XP crystals to level up, and unlock pirate weapons from a Straw Hat flintlock to a golden Kizaru laser beam and Blackbeard’s Dark Hole, which absorbs and defeats up to four Marines per hit. Grab a meat feast for ten seconds of Gear 5 invincibility.

Enter a username before setting sail. Each username’s highest round is saved in the same browser; these records are not shared across devices.

### Controls

- **WASD / arrows:** Move
- **Mouse:** Aim
- **Hold left click / X:** Shoot
- **1–7:** Switch unlocked weapons
- **P:** Pause

When the mouse is outside the arena, shooting targets the closest Marine. Touch controls are available on mobile.
